# Official Data Request — Unified Coding Guide for Administrative Regions, Governorates and Centers

**To:** Data or service team, National Center for Archives and Records (NCAR)  
**Subject:** Request for a structured digital copy and terms of use for the unified administrative coding guide

Dear NCAR Data/Service Team,

We operate an information platform that aggregates officially published investment opportunities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We seek an official administrative reference to normalize opportunity locations and link regions to governorates, then to cities or administrative centers when a verified reference is available. The reference will be used only to normalize and validate locations and display official names in platform filters. We will not alter official boundaries or create administrative classifications not issued by the competent authority.

Please provide, or direct us to the custodian of, the complete current dataset corresponding to **«الدليل الموحد لترميز المناطق والمحافظات والمراكز الإدارية»** (Unified Coding Guide for Regions, Governorates and Administrative Centers), and clarify the following:

1. A complete copy of the guide and a machine-readable version (CSV, XLSX, JSON, GeoJSON, or documented API) covering regions, governorates, and administrative centers.
2. The dataset release/version identifier or issue date, effective date, and last-updated date.
3. Update frequency and change-management process, including change notifications and availability of previous versions.
4. Whether published codes are official stable identifiers across releases, and how renaming, reassignment, transfer, merger, split, retirement, or establishment of a unit is handled.
5. Explicit hierarchical links in the data: **Region → Governorate → Administrative Center**, using parent IDs or official parent codes rather than names or row order.
6. A completeness statement and record counts for regions, governorates, and administrative centers, confirming the administrative position represented and its effective date.
7. The total number of governorates represented by the current effective version. If possible, please clarify how this administrative count relates to GASTAT publications that report 150 or 151 governorates, or refer us to the authority responsible for that determination.
8. Dataset-specific licensing or permission terms and attribution requirements. Please confirm whether the data may be:
   - Stored in an internal/private platform database.
   - Used in an investment-opportunities information platform, including organizational or commercial use of the platform.
   - Used for geographic normalization, matching, and association of records with official identifiers.
   - Displayed as official region/governorate names and codes in user filters.
   - Cached and retained historically for audit and version reconciliation.
   - Redistributed only as part of derived application output, without republishing the full source file unless expressly permitted.
9. Whether a separate permit or license is required for this use, and whether it is distinct from permission to reuse the dataset.

High-resolution boundary geometry is not required for Phase 1. A complete region/governorate register with explicit parent relationships is sufficient. City/center details and geometry can be considered separately if available under suitable terms.

The concise technical specification is available in [REGION-GOVERNORATE-MINIMUM-DATA-SPEC.md](REGION-GOVERNORATE-MINIMUM-DATA-SPEC.md). If NCAR is not the data custodian or authority for reuse permission, please direct us to the appropriate official authority or submission channel.

Respectfully,

**Investment Opportunities Information Platform Team**
