# Official Data and Reuse-License Request — Geographical Names and Administrative Reference

**To:** Data/geospatial services team, General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA)  
**Subject:** Request for a structured Geographical Names dataset and confirmation of its specific reuse license

Dear GEOSA Data/Geospatial Services Team,

We operate an information platform that aggregates officially published investment opportunities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We seek an official geographic reference to normalize opportunity locations and link regions to governorates, then to cities or administrative centers where a documented source is available. The reference will be used only for normalization, matching, validation, and display of official names in platform filters. We will not alter official boundaries or represent unofficial administrative classifications.

Please provide a link or copy of the **“Geographical Names”** dataset published through GEOSA Open Data and clarify the following:

1. The **specific license applicable to the “Geographical Names” dataset**, including the license text, version, effective date, and URL or copy. Please distinguish it from general portal terms if those terms differ from the dataset license.
2. Whether the license permits use in an internal/private database and in an investment-opportunities information platform, including organizational or commercial use of the platform.
3. Whether it permits transformation/normalization and matching of names, linking records to identifiers, display of names/codes in the application and search filters, caching, and historical retention for audit.
4. Whether derived application output may be redistributed, and any limits on redistributing source data or extracted records. Please provide the required attribution wording.
5. A complete structured, record-level dataset for regions, governorates, and administrative centers, and cities where officially represented, in CSV, XLSX, JSON, GeoJSON, or a documented API.
6. The available fields, including region ID/code and Arabic/English name; governorate ID/code and Arabic/English name plus parent region ID/code; and city/center ID/code, name, type, and parent governorate and region IDs.
7. Whether identifiers are official and stable across releases; explicit Region → Governorate → City/Center relationships; and the rules for changes, retirement, and renaming.
8. Dataset release/version, effective date, last-updated date, update frequency, change-management policy, and availability of historical versions.
9. Record counts and a nationwide completeness statement for each administrative level, including the region and governorate totals represented in the effective version.
10. Boundary/geometry data if available, with coordinate reference system, license, version, and terms. High-resolution geometry is not required for Phase 1 region/governorate filters and may be supplied as a separate package.
11. Whether this use requires any separate license or permit. Please explicitly distinguish **permission to reuse an open dataset** from a **license to conduct a geospatial economic/professional activity**. We do not assume one grants the other.

The data will be used to normalize and validate opportunity locations and prevent incorrect geographic assignment. We will not modify official boundaries or create alternate administrative classifications.

The [concise technical specification](REGION-GOVERNORATE-MINIMUM-DATA-SPEC.md) is available for reference. If another authority holds the data or grants the applicable permission, please direct us to the appropriate official authority and submission route.

Respectfully,

**Investment Opportunities Information Platform Team**
