• Location (Region): Al-Ahsa Governate, Eastern Region
• Accessibility from population centers (within Al-Hofuf and ~160 km 
from Dammam), allows for a large, serviceable market of 
residents/tourists
• High accessibility driven by developed roads and infrastructure
• The proposed site already has visibility among locals as evidenced by 
existing visitorship to Al-Ahsa National Park
• On-site vegetation allows operators to provide various outdoor, 
nature-based activities
• Growing investments into sustainable tourism projects 
(e.g., AMAALA) suggest that ecotourism is expected to 
grow in popularity
• Plans for ecotourism destinations in the Eastern Region 
specifically are also gaining traction (e.g., TDF’s3 Half 
Moon Beach Resort announced in 2024)
• The Eastern Province has seen a ~377% growth in 
tourists and a 280% rise in spending from ’21 to ’23
• The global ecotourism market is seeing strong growth, valued at $185.87 billion USD as of 2021 and expected to grow at a 15.2% CAGR until 
2030, with market tailwinds including:
• Rising demand for nature-based tourism: nature-based tourism constitutes a significant portion of global tourism market, with rapid growth 
observed over past decade
• Increased accessibility through enhanced polices: many countries are implementing more relaxed visa policies to boost tourism (e.g., Saudi 
Arabia has introduced an eVisa system, offering a one-year, multiple-entry visa, allowing tourists to spend up to 90 days in the country, thereby 
facilitating increased international visitation)
